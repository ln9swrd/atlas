### Root Motion 요구사항
* **In‑Place** – The current `CombatComponent` does not use root motion for movement; it relies on a sphere‑trace and movement component for displacement.  Therefore, the animation should be *in‑place* to avoid conflicting movement.
* If root motion is used, it must be **restricted** so that the character’s world position changes by less than `AttackRange` (150 cm) during the attack window.
* Root motion that affects orientation is acceptable if it is a natural swing of the upper body.
